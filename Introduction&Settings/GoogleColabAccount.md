## Simple steps to access and set up your environment:

## 1) Log In to Google Colab
  Open your browser and go to the official Google Colab website (https://colab.research.google.com/).
  Click Sign in in the top right corner.
  Enter your existing Google account credentials. If you do not have one yet, you can create a free account by clicking Create account.
## 2) Create Your First Notebook
  Once you log in, a welcome pop-up window will appear:
  Click the New notebook button at the bottom of the pop-up to open a blank workspace.
  Your new notebook will be automatically saved to your personal Google Drive storage.
## 3) Alternative Method: Access via Google Drive
  If you prefer organizing your projects directly within your cloud folders, you can connect Colab to your Drive:
  Go to Google Drive.
  Click New in the top-left corner, hover over More, and select Connect more apps.
  Search for "Google Colaboratory" in the search bar and click Install.
  Now you can create a new notebook anywhere in your Drive by right-clicking, selecting More, and choosing Google Colaboratory.
